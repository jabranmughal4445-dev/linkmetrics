Privacy Policy

Last updated 5 October 2026. This is a plain-language summary of how this page behaves. It is not legal advice.

What you enter

You can enter a website URL, anchor text and a target URL. These are used only to produce the analysis you see on screen.

Where processing happens

The analysis runs in your browser, using the JavaScript on this page. The page's code does not send what you enter to a server and does not call an analysis service. When you run an analysis, the website URL you entered is sent to this site's free fetch function, which requests that page from its website and returns the text. That site can see the request. Nothing is stored. If the page cannot be fetched, no result is produced. Your input and results are not saved, so they disappear when you refresh or close the page.

What is saved on your device

Only your appearance choices are saved, using your browser's local storage: light or dark mode, the selected preset and any custom colors. You can remove them with Reset Theme or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Clipboard

The copy buttons write to your clipboard only when you click them. The page never reads your clipboard.

Requests made by your browser

  • The page's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. As with any font request, the font provider can see standard request details such as your IP address and browser type.
  • The server that delivers this page can likewise see standard request details. Neither of these includes the text you enter into the tool. The website URL you enter is requested from that website only.
  • The page's own code does not set cookies and does not include analytics or advertising scripts.

Your care with content

What you enter stays in your browser tab, but avoid entering confidential URLs on a shared or public computer, because your browser may retain them.

Changes

If the way this page handles information changes, this policy will be updated and the date above will change.

What the tool does

LinkMetrics by Asma Amir reads the web page you enter and suggests where a link with your anchor text and target URL could fit most naturally and how to place it.

Suggestions are recommendations

Scores, placements and anchor text are estimates produced by simple text-matching rules applied to the content you provide. They are suggestions, not decisions. Review every suggestion yourself before you use it, and judge whether the link is genuinely useful to the reader. Nothing here guarantees that a link will be accepted by a site owner, valued by a search engine or lead to any ranking or traffic result.

Your responsibilities

  • Get permission from the site owner or editor before adding or requesting a link in their content.
  • Follow the policies of the site where the link will appear and the guidelines of the search engines you care about.
  • Mark paid or sponsored links with the appropriate link attributes and disclosures where required.
  • Do not use the tool to create deceptive, spammy or manipulative placements.

Quality of results

Results depend on the text the page returns to your browser. Pages that block automated access, or that load their text with JavaScript, cannot be analyzed, and no result is given for them.

No professional advice

The tool does not provide legal, marketing or search engine optimization advice.
